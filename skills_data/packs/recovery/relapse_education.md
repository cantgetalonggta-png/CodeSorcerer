# Relapse Education Support

## Scope
Educational framing only. Not diagnosis, not treatment, not a substitute for licensed care.

## Useful structure for support materials
1. Define lapse vs relapse in plain language.
2. Common trigger categories (environmental, emotional, social) as educational lists.
3. Coping plan template fields (warning signs, people to contact, safe activities).
4. Encourage professional and peer support resources.

Always include a clear disclaimer that this is educational content only.
